THE ROADMAP

From task to done — fast.

A clear, OTP-verified flow for both sides of the marketplace.

If you need help (Poster)

  1. Post in 60s — describe the task & set a budget.
  2. Negotiate fast — timed offers from nearby workers.
  3. Secure escrow — pay safely; funds are held.
  4. Verify & rate — use OTP on completion to release payment.

If you’re ready to work (Worker)

  1. Find nearby — live map of tasks around you.
  2. Offer & win — accept budget or send counter-offer.
  3. Do the job — verify arrival with OTP; complete the task.
  4. Get paid instantly — money hits wallet post-OTP.
