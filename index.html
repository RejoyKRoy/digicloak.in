How DigiCloak came together

Not a franchise, and not a single generalist wearing three hats.

DigiCloak started the way most useful things do — a group of friends who had already spent their careers doing this work separately, for other people's companies, and realised the businesses they knew best were badly served by vendors who could only do one part of the job well.

Beyond that founding team, we've built a trusted network of working professionals around us — many holding full-time roles inside corporate companies, in the exact field a given engagement needs. We bring them in project by project, not as a permanent bench. That means the person testing your application or scoping your audit has current, on-the-ground experience, not a title borrowed for the proposal. Every engagement is handled by a specialist in that specific field, not a generalist account manager relaying it to a subcontractor.

Because the team and network work remote-first, DigiCloak doesn't carry the overhead of a large office footprint or a permanent bench of consultants — and we pass that saving on. The aim is simple: reliable, affordable, world-class professional services, quoted transparently with no hidden markup, so proper procurement, security and compliance work is within reach of a growing business, not just an enterprise with a compliance budget line.

What is a CERT-In empanelled audit and when does my business need one?

A CERT-In audit is an independent cyber security assessment carried out by an auditing organisation empanelled by the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team under Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000. Sectoral regulators including the RBI, SEBI, IRDAI and UIDAI require regulated entities to be audited by an empanelled auditor, and many enterprise customers and government tenders ask for the same report before onboarding a vendor. DigiCloak scopes the audit, prepares your systems and evidence, and delivers the engagement together with a CERT-In empanelled auditing organisation.

Can DigiCloak supply hardware and secure it under one contract?

Yes. Procurement, cybersecurity and audit run as three departments inside one company, so the laptops and servers we source arrive already imaged, encrypted, enrolled in endpoint protection and recorded in your asset register. That removes the usual gap between the reseller who sold the machine and the auditor who later asks who owns it.

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?

For most small and mid-sized businesses a first-time ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification takes roughly three to six months from the initial gap assessment to the certification audit, depending on how much of the information security management system already exists and how quickly evidence can be collected.

What is the difference between SOC 2 and ISO 27001?

ISO/IEC 27001 is an internationally recognised certification against a formal information security management system standard. SOC 2 is an attestation report assessed against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria, and is more commonly requested by US and global SaaS buyers. Many businesses pursue both, and a large share of the underlying controls and documentation overlap.

What does a VAPT engagement with DigiCloak cover?

A VAPT engagement covers vulnerability assessment and manual penetration testing across the assets you put in scope: web applications, mobile applications, APIs, internal and external networks, and cloud environments. You receive a prioritised findings report with reproduction steps and business impact, remediation guidance for your engineering team, and a retest to confirm fixes before a clearance certificate is issued.
