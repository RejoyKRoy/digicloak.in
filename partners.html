Multi-Vendor Synergy

Enabling intelligent connectivity through top-tier ISP and infrastructure ecosystems across India.

Our Collaboration Model

DIGICLOAK is not directly affiliated with any single internet or telecom provider. Instead, we work across a broad spectrum of Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors to deliver the most efficient, redundant, and cost-effective infrastructure solutions tailored to your needs. By harnessing partnerships with premium ISPs and data infrastructure enablers in India, we ensure optimized delivery of broadband, leased line, MPLS, SD-WAN, SIP trunks, VPN, and more.

We specialize in combining strengths from multiple leading Indian telecom brands to design reliable, failover-ready architectures. From high-speed business broadband in metro cities to rural fiber deployments and secure voice gateways for enterprises, our vendor-agnostic model ensures your business never relies on a single point of failure.

Types of Partners We Integrate

  • Tier 1 ISPs with nationwide fiber backbone
  • Tier 2 regional ISPs with flexible pricing models
  • Cloud exchange & DC providers with edge nodes
  • Security vendors for firewall, DDoS, and compliance solutions
  • Smart office integrators offering surveillance and automation
  • CRM, VoIP, and IPPBX service aggregators

Through strategic alignment with such entities, DIGICLOAK offers a unified platform for sourcing, deploying, and managing all your digital connectivity needs without vendor lock-in.
