Service Offerings

We bring the best of Tier 1 and Tier 2 ISPs together for seamless, efficient and cost-effective technology enablement across India.

1. Connectivity Solutions

  • Dedicated Internet Access (DIA): 1:1 uncontended leased line circuits for businesses requiring high SLAs and uptime.
  • Broadband + Static IP: High-speed SME/SMB broadband with dedicated IPv4 or IPv6 IP pools.
  • MPLS/SD-WAN: Fully managed network overlay for branch-level interconnectivity with dynamic routing & QoS.
  • Point-to-Point/Point-to-Multipoint: Private network solutions for enterprises with inter-office bandwidth demands.

2. Voice & Telephony

  • PRI (Primary Rate Interface): Digital voice trunk for PBX and call center deployments with scalability.
  • SIP Trunks: VoIP-based cost-effective trunking with caller masking and IVR enablement.
  • Cloud Telephony: CRM-integrated cloud PBX with call routing, missed call alerts, and agent monitoring dashboards.

3. Smart Office IT Infrastructure

  • Firewall & Security Appliances: Fortinet, Sophos, Palo Alto Next-gen Firewall (NGFW) deployments for perimeter security.
  • Routers/Switches/Wi-Fi 6: Aruba, Cisco Meraki, Netgear, TP-Link commercial grade setup for full coverage zones.
  • AMC + IT Helpdesk: Proactive support and device lifecycle management with reporting & asset audits.

4. Cloud & Hosting

  • Domain + Web Hosting: .in/.com domains with managed cPanel or static GitHub hosting setup.
  • Mail Servers: Enterprise emails with SPF, DKIM, DMARC compliance and SMTP whitelisting.
  • Website Development: Responsive websites built with HTML/CSS/JS for lightweight, SEO-first experience.

5. IoT & Surveillance

  • GPS + Fleet Tracking: Real-time vehicle telemetry with driver behavior analytics and dashboard alerts.
  • Surveillance & NVR: Hikvision, CP Plus, Dahua CCTV + remote monitoring over LTE/FTTH/IP broadband.
  • Smart Sensors: Motion, door, and temperature sensors integrated with cloud dashboards.

6. Digital Marketing & CRM

  • Google Business & SEO: Full setup, reviews management, and local visibility campaigns for SMBs.
  • Social Media + Meta Ads: Targeted Instagram/Facebook ads with A/B testing and reach optimization.
  • CRM Integration: WhatsApp, SMS and inbound call capture integrated into lightweight CRMs.
